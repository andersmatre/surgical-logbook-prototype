# Logbook test: instructions for testers

Thank you for helping. This is a test version of a new logbook app. We are testing the app, not you. If something is confusing, that is the app's fault, and we want to hear about it.

Please do not type patient names or numbers anywhere in the app. Your entries stay on your tablet until you choose to send the export file.

## Set-up (5 minutes, internet needed once)

1. Open the link we sent you in Chrome.
2. Chrome menu (three dots), then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
3. Open the app from your home screen. Enter the tester code we gave you (for example S01) and choose your hospital.

## Tasks

Please try these in order. Do not ask anyone for help. If you get stuck, note where.

1. Log the last procedure you did. (Log tab: tap the procedure, then tap your role.)
2. Log two more procedures, one of them from "More procedures".
3. Make a mistake on purpose and undo it.
4. Switch on airplane mode. Log two procedures. Switch airplane mode off and tap the button at the top right.
5. Open "Progress" and look at your numbers. Send the weekly summary to yourself on WhatsApp.
6. Ask a supervisor to sign your entries. (History tab, "Supervisor sign-off". Demo PINs: Supervisor A = 1111, Supervisor B = 2222.)

## Use it for real for one to two weeks

Log your procedures in the app when you do them (you can keep your normal logbook as well). We want to see whether it is easy enough that you actually use it.

## Then

1. Open the app, More tab, "Export test data". Send the file to us on WhatsApp.
2. Answer these questions (voice message is fine):
   - What was confusing or slow?
   - What was missing?
   - Compared with the logbook you used before: easier, the same, or harder? Why?
   - What would make you use a logbook every day?
   - Would your supervisor use the sign-off? What would stop them?

Tip for us: if you can, record your screen while doing the tasks (swipe down from the top, "Screen recorder") and send us the video. It helps a lot.
