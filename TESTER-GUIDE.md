# Logbook test: instructions for testers

Thank you for helping. This is a test version of a new logbook app. We are testing the app, not you. If something is confusing, that is the app's fault, and we want to hear about it.

## Set-up (5 minutes, internet needed)

1. Open the link we sent you in Chrome.
2. Chrome menu (three dots), then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
3. Open the app from your home screen.
4. Log in with your **candidate number** and the **password you were given**.
5. The app asks you to choose **your own password**. Choose one only you know, write it down somewhere safe, and do not share it with anyone, also not your supervisor.
6. Choose the hospital where you work now.

You need internet for the first login on a tablet. After that the logbook also works without internet.

## Tasks

Please try these in order. Do not ask anyone for help. If you get stuck, note where.

1. Log the last procedure you did. (the "New case" tab: tap the procedure, tap your role, answer the questions you know, tap Save. If some are not answered, the app asks: tap "Save anyway" or "Go back".)
2. Log two more procedures, one of them from "More operations" and one under Delivery.
3. Make a mistake on purpose and undo it.
4. Switch on airplane mode. Log two procedures. Switch airplane mode off. Watch the top right: while cases wait it shows "Offline" or "N to sync"; after a short time the button disappears, which means everything is sent.
5. Open "Progress" and look at your numbers.
6. Add a follow-up to one of your operations: what happened to the patient afterwards. Open "History", tap the case, tap "Follow-up", then "Add follow-up". (At the top of History the app says how many cases can still get a follow-up.)
7. Ask your supervisor to add your candidate number in their app and sign your cases. Your cases must be synced first (no sync button is shown at the top right). When your supervisor has signed, switch to another app and back (or turn the screen off and on): the signatures appear in the History tab, and your supervisor's name appears under More, "Your supervisors".

## Use it for real for one to two weeks

Log your procedures in the app when you do them (you can keep your normal logbook as well). We want to see whether it is easy enough that you actually use it.

Your cases are sent to the server automatically when there is internet. If the button at the top right says that cases are waiting, connect to the internet for a minute.

Do not log out unless we ask you to. Logging out removes your cases from the tablet (they stay safe on the server). If the app warns you that something has not been sent, say "Cancel" and connect to the internet first.

If you forget your password, tell us. We cannot read it, but we can give you a new one.

## Then

Answer these questions (voice message is fine):
   - What was confusing or slow?
   - What was missing?
   - Was logging in easy? Would you manage it without help?
   - Compared with the logbook you used before: easier, the same, or harder? Why?
   - What would make you use a logbook every day?
   - Did the signing work for you and your supervisor? What would stop your supervisor from using it?

Tip for us: if you can, record your screen while doing the tasks (swipe down from the top, "Screen recorder") and send us the video. It helps a lot.

## For supervisors

You need internet for all of this (the supervisor screens do not work offline).

1. Open the link in Chrome, add it to the Home screen, and open it from there.
2. Log in with your **candidate number** and the password you were given. Choose your own password and keep it to yourself.
3. Tap **Add students**, find the candidate numbers of the students you supervise and tap **Add**. The student can see your name, so nobody is added in secret.
4. Tap **My students**. A student with cases waiting shows "to sign". Tap the student.
5. Look at the list under **To sign**. Nothing is ticked at first. **Tick the cases you supervised** ("Select all" ticks every one), then tap **Sign**. Only sign procedures you supervised and that are correct. A signature cannot be undone.
6. Tell us:
   - What was confusing or slow?
   - Would you do this after each operation, once a day or once a week? Where would you be when you do it?
   - Did you have internet when you wanted to sign? If not, how often does that happen?
   - Is it right that you can add students yourself, or should someone else decide?
   - What is missing?

Tip: if you can, record your screen while doing this (swipe down from the top, "Screen recorder") and send us the video.

## For administrators (CapaCare staff)

You need internet. You never need a command line for this.

1. Log in with your candidate number (for example `ADMIN-ANNA`) and choose your own password.
2. **Add people:** **Manage users**, then **Add users**. Choose Students, Supervisor or Administrator. For students, type one per line: the candidate number, then the name (you can paste a list). Tap **Create accounts**.
3. **Hand out the passwords:** the new initial passwords are shown **once**. Write them down or tap **Copy all**. Give each person their own, through CapaCare, one at a time, not in a group chat. They choose their own password at the first login.
4. **Forgotten password:** **Manage users**, tap the person, **New password**. They get a new initial password, shown once.
5. **Someone leaves:** tap the person, **Disable**. Their cases stay. To erase a person who asks for it: disable first, then "Delete account and data for good".
6. **Lost or stolen tablet:** tap the person, **Log out everywhere**.
7. **Backup, at least once a week:** **More**, **Download backup**. Keep the file somewhere safe that is not the server, and do not send it by WhatsApp or e-mail. The Overview reminds you when a week has passed.
8. **Spreadsheets:** **Download data**. Choose Anonymous (codes instead of names) unless you really need the names, tick the files and tap **Download**. The **Codebook** explains every column.
9. Tell us:
   - What was confusing or slow?
   - What do you need to do that the app does not let you do?
